Every folder `NN-creator-id/` holds: `video.mp4` (small, 540p or 576p), `info.json` (trimmed), `transcript.txt`
(whisper small.en, timestamps), `hook.txt` (first ~3 s spoken + what is on screen in frame 1), `sheet.jpg`
(frames at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3 s, then every 4 s; every 6-10 s for long videos).
Stats were read on 2026-11-02 and keep moving; several videos were only 2-7 days old, so low numbers can just mean "new".
Save rate = saves / views (the best single signal of "useful enough to keep"; 5 % and up is strong).
