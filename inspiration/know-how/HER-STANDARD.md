# Her standard: the rules every video gets (one page; read FIRST, every video, every helper)

Why this file exists (her words, 2026-11-12): "when I give a comment about one video, it completely disappears. I have to say
the same thing in another video. It's so annoying." Every note she gives on ONE video is a rule for ALL videos. When she
gives a new note: add it here the same day (one line, plain), then FEEDBACK.md for the detail. Every helper brief starts
with "Read inspiration/know-how/HER-STANDARD.md".

## Thumbnails
- Use the board: studio /ideas/boards/thumbnails/ (180+ items, "Thumbnails N"; images in inspiration/boards/thumbnails/img/).
  Pick 10-15 DIFFERENT board items and copy each one's composition (layout, picture kind, type style) for this video. Never
  one formula for the set (no more "her cut-out right + heavy caps left" every time; max 2 per set). Review sheet: each
  thumbnail beside the board item it copies (outputs/_review/thumbnails-vs-board.jpg).
- Words a stranger understands AND wants: what the video gives them, in their words ("Watch this before applying to one more
  job", "STOP WASTING TIME", "WEB3 EXPLAINED"). Never a story detail from inside the video (no "$12,000 in fines", "O'Brien",
  "29 Feb 2012"). Search keywords people use go in (e.g. "fully funded scholarships", "2027", "Nigerian students"); a niche
  term ("edge cases") goes in the TITLE, the thumbnail uses the plain phrase ("bugs that break your app").
- Words on photos must pop: darken the room behind the words, her cut-out natural on top, strong shadow. Her face natural,
  never brightened. Her shoot photos for the video's outfit (library/her-photos/shoot-2026-10-29/README.md).
- Pick board formats whose PICTURE carries the topic (a chart, a map, a split with two numbers, her pointing at a yes/no list):
  copies of template formats ("X EXPLAINED" on flat colour, a logo/flag grid, a "RANKED" label) scored 2-6 with cold viewers (2026-11-12).

## Short covers (the first 0.1 s of every short)
- Her line breaks are law: a new line she types in the studio (Shift+Enter) is a real line break on covers, titles and labels;
  never re-wrap her lines (only shrink to fit). Default cover lines are set by hand too ("PROOF / OF FUNDS") (2026-11-12).
- Plain AND targeted: name the thing the audience searches ("What is a race condition?", "Hacking Starbucks gift cards with a
  race condition", "Ever been charged twice for one order? (duplicate submission)", "Null errors in production"). Never a
  "he/she" nobody can place, never jargon-free mush that hides who it is for, never the same text as the top label twice.
- A cover can be a big line alone (cover "big": true) or a slide from the short under the line (cover style "frame").
- Name the audience on most covers ("for Nigerians", "Nigerian students"), pick a still with her eyes open, and mix the cover kinds in one set
  (big line / her photo / cut-out card / real page), never two neighbours alike (cold critic, cheapest-countries 2026-11-12).

## Shorts
- A MIX of formats from the shorts formats board (studio /ideas/formats/, "Formats N", inspiration/short-formats/formats.json
  + frames/): mixed fonts + cuts + inserts (62), talking head + pop-up screenshots (66), white serif captions on black boxes
  (67), black/white rounded caption boxes, letterbox, full-screen... No two neighbours alike, one format at most ~1 in 4.
  Name the board number each short copies.
- Zoom: no zoom that just SITS on her (her whole head filling the screen). Zooms are fine as a quick effect at a moment
  (punch in on a key word, then back out). Full-screen framing itself is fine.
- Visuals land on her words: a graphic (e.g. "O'Brien") appears when she SAYS it, never sitting there before.
- Speech: start where the idea starts (no long intro she didn't need); cut asides; each short stands alone.
- Top label = the short's topic in plain words, inside the frame (labels must not fall into the picture).
- Mix levels she likes: music about -14 to -15, sound effects about -7 to -8 (studio sliders); master -14 LUFS.

## Teaser (v3, TEASER-PLAYBOOK §0a)
- A stranger can FOLLOW it: one relatable stretch kept whole as the hook -> the idea in one line -> 3-5 examples, each a
  complete mini-thought that she NAMES out loud ("the duplicate submission", "a race condition"), with its setup ("say we have
  one item left in stock") -> her takeaway -> the ending. Trailer energy stays (cuts shown, title cards, music drop).

## Ending ("Full video on YouTube")
- Her own design (cream ground, black panel, camera circle, FULL VIDEO ON YOUTUBE in Poppins Bold, thumbnail, title card,
  "with Gbemi" + Subscribed + bell, Like / Comment / Share). Default in layouts.js; uses the video's recommended thumbnail.

## Edits
- Retakes, "scratch that", wrong facts: cut them cleanly (check with a transcript of the new sound). No correction cards if
  the mistake can simply be cut.

## Working with her
- She exports unless she asks us to ("download them into my Downloads"): then hard-link into one folder per video.
- Plain words, links to the studio editors, say where files land. Start the studio.

## Added 2026-11-12 (night): shorts, covers, captions (she redid the brief because the same mistakes came back)
- EVERY short: its cover style and caption style each reference AT LEAST THREE board formats (studio /ideas/formats/,
  "Formats N"); the FONTS come from one of those board videos (match the look: e.g. Formats 32 @aishahwasim = rounded
  heavy lime word + clean small lowercase captions; Formats 26 @mariahtyesha = huge cream/yellow condensed caps stacked
  over her + single bold white lowercase words). Write the 3 references per short in the manifest ("board": [n, n, n]) and
  the kit. No style we invented (no bent/curved text, no random script overlays) unless a referenced board video has it.
- Never two text layers on top of each other (a caption over a title, a script line over caps). Titles/labels NEVER fall
  into the picture: they fit inside the top band (auto-shrink to 2 lines max) in every short.
- Clean beats busy: fewer fonts (2 max per short), clear hierarchy, like Formats 32.
- Cover lines in the words people type on TikTok/YouTube, short and natural, not "X: Y" research-paper titles and not
  the same pattern on every short: "Proof of funds for Nigerian students" (+ Nigerian flag), "Free tuition for
  international students in Germany", "Studying in France 2027", "Italy for Nigerian students", "Is tuition free for
  Nigerians in Austria?". Check TikTok/YouTube search wording before writing them.
- Covers with her: HER in her real room background (a real frame), not her cut-out on a plain colour.
- No colour she has rejected (the red "What is PROOF OF FUNDS?" style).

## Fonts (her rule 2026-11-12: "Only use fonts from our boards")
- Use ONLY fonts from the font board (studio /ideas/boards/fonts/, "Font N"; fonts found on her idea boards); design-board fonts first;
  never one marked 'Not for me' (inspiration/boards/fonts/picks.json, pick "no"). Fonts we added ourselves that aren't on the board are not to be used.
- Colour pairs come from the font board's colour combos and the design board (ground / ink / accent of a named "Design N").
- Cite the font-board number(s) in every brief and manifest (e.g. "fonts: Font 12 + Font 37").
- Use a board font THE WAY IT WAS USED on its source (her note 2026-11-12): the same font + the same text colour + the same
  background colour as the board item it comes from (the font-board specimen shows that pairing). Don't take a font and
  put it in our own colours.
- Readability wins: if a board font's source colours are low-contrast at video/phone size, pick another font-board item whose own pairing reads clearly (2026-11-13).
- Before showing any thumbnail set: make a 320 px sheet and COUNT layouts; if more than 2 are words/card left + her right, rebuild before reporting (2026-11-13: it slipped through twice).

## Captions and posts (her rule 2026-11-16, the edge-cases LinkedIn post is the model)
- Every caption and post (YouTube description opening, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, Shorts descriptions, community posts)
  is CONVERSATIONAL, like talking to a friend: flowing spoken sentences, "right?", her asides and brackets, a personal
  opener ("One of the things I love the most about…"), what she actually did ("I've been reading a lot about… so I made a
  video"), her takeaway in her words. No list of what the video covers, no story details stacked as proof. Model:
  projects/the-edge-case-you-think-is-impossible/kit/refined-kit.md section 6a. Read HER-VOICE.md Part A too.
