# Thumbnail study: what her saved thumbnails do that ours don't (2026-11-09)

Source: the 131 pictures on the thumbnail board (studio `/ideas/boards/thumbnails/`: 43 Pinterest pins, 71 YouTube screenshots
with titles, her own 2, her viral samples) + 13 she pasted on 2026-11-09 (the art of offline living, 48 HOURS, I'D FAIL SLAVERY,
Living in Finland diaries, JUST WAIT, small apartment hacks, PhD Vlog, Reading as an Act of Resistance, my potential collage,
"i can't cope if i'm not reading", I DID 1 YEAR OF PERSONAL CURRICULUMS, BECOME DANGEROUSLY ARTICULATE, upgrade your life with
neuroplasticity). Her verdict on ours: "not in any way on the level of any of these".
Read with THUMBNAILS.md (rules) and the board's picks.json (her likes). Design references: `/ideas/boards/design/`.

## 1. The words: a stranger understands them with NO video
- Every reference's words make sense cold: a topic ("PhD Vlog", "small apartment hacks", "WEB3 EXPLAINED!", "ALGORITHMS MADE
  EASY", "UI UX Complete Roadmap", "Living in Finland diaries"), a time or number ("48 HOURS", "I DID 1 YEAR OF PERSONAL
  CURRICULUMS!"), or a plain claim ("Reading as an Act of Resistance", "BECOME DANGEROUSLY ARTICULATE", "JUST WAIT").
- Ours failed this: "Hold on / OK / Moved / You / Server", "201 = Created", "4XX = YOU", "SAID 200. FAILED." are shorthand from
  inside the video. Her words (2026-11-09): "I have not watched the video. How would I know what the hell that even means?"
- **No labels on things.** References have ONE line of text (sometimes a tiny second line in brackets: "(Mathematics &
  Astrophysics)"). Never five small captions under five boxes.
- Numbers a stranger knows (404, 500, 200 OK) can be pictures; numbers only insiders know (201, 422, 3xx) cannot carry a thumbnail.

## 2. Thumbnail + title are one pair; the picture predicts the title
- The title is never the thumbnail text again. Patterns:
  - thumbnail = theme / genre, title = the personal story: "the art of offline living" + "it really was the damn phone: i stopped
    scrolling and remembered who i was"; "Living in Finland diaries" + "An ordinary day in my life in Finland…".
  - thumbnail = the big claim, title = what it is: "BECOME DANGEROUSLY ARTICULATE" + "Psychology of Verbal Intelligence: How To
    Articulate Your Thoughts"; "Reading as an Act of Resistance" + "Why Are Harvard Students Lining Up for This 'Brutal' Class?".
  - thumbnail = the hook in 1-2 words, title = the full promise: "48 HOURS" + "giving myself 48 hours to do everything I've been
    putting off"; "JUST WAIT" + "please stop rushing".
  - tech: thumbnail = the topic name huge + made easy / explained / roadmap, title = the specific outcome ("Top 7 Algorithms for
    Coding Interviews Explained" + "ALGORITHMS MADE EASY").
- So we write the PAIR first: the picture (what she is doing / holding / where), the 1-5 words, the title. Then design.

## 3. The picture: a real scene of the topic, not a diagram
- Kinds that repeat (use a MIX per set):
  a. **Real scene of the topic being lived**, text big across it: vacuuming for 48 HOURS, library shelves for PhD Vlog, the bride for
     JUST WAIT, apartment rooms for small apartment hacks. No face needed to be big.
  b. **Photo collage panels (2-4 real photos) + one centred line**: Living in Finland diaries, the art of offline living, POV, A
     PORTLAND VLOG, adulting habits. Serif or soft sans, white, no box.
  c. **Creator in the middle surrounded by real objects of the topic** (curriculum notebooks, brain scans + books, small photo
     cut-outs "my potential"): the objects ARE the topic, big and many but tidy.
  d. **Creator large on one side + a bold claim on the other** (Reading as an Act of Resistance with a red underline, I'D FAIL
     SLAVERY, BECOME DANGEROUSLY ARTICULATE, just start.): 2-4 words, heavy, one word coloured or underlined.
  e. **Tech explainer**: the topic name huge + the literal thing (logo grid for DevOps tools, code for "stop using div", a notepad
     sketch for Two Sum, a roadmap). Clean dark or light ground, 1 accent colour.
  f. **Before / after or day 1 / day 900 split** only when the topic IS a change.
- Faces: calm, natural, often doing something or looking at the thing; never forced shock. Her face sharp and natural-bright
  (Enhance photo, skin natural, no extra light on a bright photo).

## 4. Type and finish
- One typeface per thumbnail, at most two (claim + small bracket line). Lowercase serif / soft sans for aesthetic ones; heavy
  uppercase sans for claims; underline or colour ONE word.
- Text sits on the photo (no panels, no boxes, no outlines), white with a soft shadow on busy photos, or dark on calm areas.
- Lots of air. 3-6 big elements at most. Readable at phone size (the board view at "Smaller" is the test).

## 5. Gate before showing her anything
1. Words alone, read cold: can a stranger name the topic? (no shorthand, no script phrase, no labels)
2. Title next to it: does it add the story / outcome without repeating the words?
3. Picture: a real scene, objects, or her doing the thing; not a chart with captions.
4. Which board reference does it follow (cite the pin id)? Does it reach that level?

## 6. Covers for Reels / TikTok / Shorts: built INTO each short as its first frames, not a thumbnail (her correction 2026-11-10; idea 2026-11-09; refs: thumbnail board group "Reel covers (Instagram grid)",
## 39 covers from her explore screenshots, originals in inspiration/instagram-2026-11-09/)
- The cover is the still people see in the profile and explore grids. Instagram shows the MIDDLE 3:4 of the 9:16 frame in the
  grid (at 1080x1920: y ≈ 240-1680 visible), the profile grid even tighter. All words and her face sit inside y ≈ 300-1100.
- What the covers do: her face mid-sentence (expressive, not posed) + 2-8 words in the top third that state the hook or the topic
  ("software engineering is dead", "Imagine not getting that internship when these literally exist", "How I got into AI Ethics",
  "STOP USING AI illegally", "The 5 AI skills companies are hiring for in 2026", "Live Coding: Backend API", "8 Mobile Menu Types").
- Type: one strong style per creator, kept across the whole series (the AI-ethics creator uses the same layout every time, so the
  grid reads as one brand); white words with a soft shadow on the photo, or a white / coloured box behind the words; one word in a
  colour or italic serif.
- Some covers are full design cards (a phone mock-up, a paper sheet, a colour grid) instead of her face, mixed in to break the
  grid. Same rule as thumbnails: a stranger understands the words without the video.

## 7. Bold colour + a quiet pattern (her refs 2026-11-09, design board group "Bold colour + quiet pattern")
- Flat BOLD grounds (pale lime-yellow, deep violet, magenta) carrying a fine static pattern in the SAME hue (engraved / moiré lines,
  halftone stripes), only a few % lighter or darker, so the colour still reads flat and bold. Her words: "the bold colour has some
  patterns that don't really overwhelm the bold colour, but they look nice."
- Wavy stamp-edged panels, one illustrated object drawn with the same line shading (padlock, key, podium crowd), heavy grotesk
  type mixed with a light weight, purple on yellow.
