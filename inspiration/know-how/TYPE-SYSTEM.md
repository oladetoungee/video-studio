## Layout rules (from her references)
- Type IS the image: headline 40-75 % of the frame, may run off one edge; big scale contrast with a small body line.
- Flat colour fields only; one accent colour per frame.
- Text sits on clean space with generous margins (≥ 6 % of the frame); body copy never touches or overlaps the headline.
- **No thick borders, outlines or strokes**: no outlined/stroked captions, no boxes around text, no stamp frames, no rings, no card borders thicker than 2 px (a 1 px hairline rule is fine). Separate with space and colour, not lines.
- **No handwriting / marker fonts** (Caveat, marker, "Hand") and no novelty fonts (Bebas, Barlow, Saira, Geist display, Onest display, Funnel, Dela Gothic, Young Serif, Bowlby, Reenie Beanie): use the six sets above.
- Captions in talking-head edits and shorts: the set's body font, solid fill, no outline; a soft shadow only if needed for contrast (no hard stroke).
- Cut-outs of her photo sit with the type (overlap is fine when deliberate), never with a white sticker border.
