# Motion references she saved (2026-10-11/12): frame study + layout menu

Six screen recordings in `inspiration/motion-references/` (all silent: screen recordings, no audio track, so no sound
study possible; the sound side is in `SOUND-PALETTE.md`). Studied with ffmpeg scene-detect + 1 fps contact sheets +
25 fps strips across transitions + pixel samples. Nothing was written to disk except small sheets in a scratch folder
(deleted). Times are seconds into each recording; recordings are 100 fps captures of a browser / phone player, so the
source fps is unknown and timings are to about 1 frame at 25-30 fps.

Her brief these feed (2026-10-12): every video gets its own peculiar video type; the talking-head edit is now FULL length
with motion in many forms (half motion / half her face, her full-frame with things popping up and moving around,
full-screen motion inserts); shorts built the way she builds them (`SHORTS-PLAYBOOK.md`); fewer whooshes, more varied
sounds.

Our video types used below: **F** pure motion film on her narration · **T** full-length talking-head with graphics ·
**S** short (9:16, 20-120 s) · **P** promo / product explainer.

## R1 · `Screen Recording 2026-10-11 at 19.56.03.mov` · "TixFox" product promo (16:9, 43.7 s)
Source: a launch film for a ticketing app "TixFox" (tixfox.co, "Start free"), from the Skillry Opus 5.5 / whatships style
of code-made promos (no creator handle on screen).
- **What it is**: a 3D-ish dithered (halftone, 1-bit pixel) fox mascot + flat colour scenes. One idea per scene, one
  line of copy on the left, one UI mockup (ticket card, price meter, phone checkout, QR ticket, payout card, sales chart)
  on the right.
- **Layout (16:9)**: copy block left at x 6-35 %, vertically centred, 3 short lines of a neutral grotesk (Helvetica /
  Inter-like, regular ~6.5 % of H); mockup right at x 55-92 %. A circular mascot avatar sits bottom-left (~9 % of H) on
  every scene as a persistent anchor. Mono corner labels top-left (`// 02 · meet tixfox`) and top-right title +
  timecode. **Her ban applies: no corner HUD in our films** (keep the idea only as a mono caption if at all).
- **Type motion**: copy types on **word by word**, each word blur-to-sharp + fade in ~4 frames, ~0.25-0.35 s apart,
  landing on the voice. The key word gets a **highlighter block** (solid accent rectangle behind black text:
  `next event?`, `TixFox.`, `costs.`, `minutes.`, `link.`, `live.`, `instantly.`, `bank.`, `phone.`, `hardware.`).
- **Mockup motion**: UI builds in place: a bar fills, a $ counter rolls up ($7,559 → $12,120 with 257 → 412 tickets), a
  chart grows bar by bar, chips stack in, a QR gets a green tick, a strike line crosses a card reader ("No hardware.").
- **Transitions**: the next ground **pushes in from the right over ~6 frames (0.2-0.25 s) with strong horizontal
  motion blur** (measured strip 9.0-9.8 s): the whole old scene smears left, new colour fills, then copy starts typing
  ~4 frames after the push settles. Cuts at 0.3, 4.1, 6.0, 9.3, 11.8, 13.8, 18.8, 22.2, 33.2, 37.0 s: one scene every
  ~3-5 s.
- **Colour**: one flat ground per scene: paper `#F0EFE8`, orange-peach, periwinkle `#8AA7EF`, lemon `#F3E435`, mint
  `#74D6B5`, sky `#74BEFC`, lilac `#C6BBFD`, off-black `#141414`; orange `#F07A2A` = the brand accent, used for the
  highlighter and the mascot. End: logo lock-up on paper with small floating ticket stickers.
- **KEEP**: word-by-word type-on with a solid highlighter on the payoff word; one UI mockup that *does something* per
  scene (counter, fill, tick); push-with-motion-blur transition; a different flat ground per beat; a persistent small
  anchor (could be her face in a circle).
- **Suits**: **P**, **F** (a "colour-per-beat" film look), and as the full-screen inserts in **T** / **S**.

## R2 · `Screen Recording 2026-10-12 at 22.34.41.mov` · @JOHNBUCOG, "how I edit captions" reel (9:16, 45.4 s)
Source: Instagram reel by **@johnbucog** (handle burned in, with an Instagram glyph top-right).
- **What it is**: a self-shot cinematic reel, almost every second a new shot (46 scene changes in 45 s: **~1 cut / s**),
  with kinetic captions as the main graphic: he talks about fonts, text placement, "subtle swooshes", sound design.
- **Captions**: heavy rounded grotesk (Black weight, white, soft drop shadow), **1-6 words on screen**, words placed
  *around* the subject (scattered, left/right of his head), with the key word **2-3x bigger** (`this`, `Next`,
  `placement`, `feel polished`, `not distracting`, `finally sound design`). Words often go **behind him** (person mask:
  "all you have been asking", "keep it classic", "colors"), some are partly hidden by a hand. One handwritten white
  scribble overlay ("how do I do this").
- **Effects**: an RGB-split glitch on the word "captions" (1-2 frames), flat white studio frames with black type for
  contrast, dark spotlight frames (a single cone of light), screen recordings of his editor (DaVinci colour wheels,
  timelines) as b-roll with one word on top.
- **Ending**: his profile grid with "now it's your turn" over it, Instagram logo pop.
- **KEEP**: text behind the person (depth) for 1-3 hero words; key word 2-3x the others; captions arranged around the
  head rather than a fixed bar for punch lines; a 1-2 frame glitch only on a word that means "digital"; flat white /
  flat black type frames between people shots.
- **Avoid copying**: 1 cut per second for a whole film (too tiring for her long talks; fine for a 20-30 s short hook).
- **Suits**: **S** (hook and punch lines), **T** for the 3-5 biggest moments per video.

- **Suits**: **T** (her full-frame stretches), **S**.

## Cross-reference: layout menu for talking-head-with-graphics (T and S)
Proportions at **16:9 1920x1080** and **9:16 1080x1920**. Times at 30 fps. Every layout follows CLAUDE.md motion rules
(arrive fast, land soft; text still >= 8 frames; nothing touches her face; never flip or recolour her footage).
"Mask" = her person matte from Apple Vision (`VNGeneratePersonSegmentationRequest`, `.accurate`), rendered once per
clip to a small matte video, never an image sequence.

**The circle (L11) is one option, never the default** (her note 2026-10-26). In a full edit no single face + graphic layout
(L5, L6, L11, L14-L19) takes more than 40 % of that time (CHECKS `layout-variety`); rotate them. Shapes stay clean: square
corners, hairline edges, flat grounds (no rounded card inside a rounded card, no glow, no gradient).

### Rhythm for a full-length talking-head edit (T)
- Something changes every **3-6 s**; no layout repeats twice in a row; at least **4 different layouts per minute**.
- Face + graphic stretches rotate L14 / L15 / L16 / L17 / L18 / L19 / L5 / L6 / L11 (none over 40 % of them; 2026-10-26).
- Rough mix per 10 minutes: L1 Talk 35-40 % · L9 Cutaway 25-30 % · L5/L6 split 10-15 % · L2/L3 giant type 5-8 % ·
  L4/L7 side stack / pop-around 8-10 % · L10 cards <= 1 per minute · L11 corner cam on long technical stretches.
- Lists ("N things", steps, chapters) -> L6 sticker on paper with the tag "01 / 02" (R5); things she names -> L4 / L7;
  her strongest claim -> L2 behind her or L10 card; a definition or diagram -> L9 cutaway from the motion film.
- Each video still gets its own look (CLAUDE.md): the menu fixes the grammar, not the palette or type.
