## The look: "A · The research wall" (her pick, 2026-10-13)

A deep-green wall covered in sticky notes and index cards, with a camera that travels along it. Picked from three directions
(A research wall, B pop-up book, C under the surface; `film/stills/directions/README.md`). Direction critic: A 6.5 then 7.5,
C 7 and 7, B 5 and 5. The opening borrows C's "dive" (a paper-and-sea cross-section the camera sinks through).

- **Ground**: wall green `#1D3A32` almost everywhere. Opening only: paper `#F3EFE6` over teal strata `#CFE7E0` `#93CDBF` `#3E9A8B` `#1F5E55`, bed `#0F2B28`.
- **Objects**: canary sticky notes `#FFE15A` (ink `#1A0F08`), orange sticky notes `#F26A2E` (deep `#B9461A`), index cards `#FFFDF6`
  with a red head rule `#E27F76` and blue lines `#C9D6EA`, tape `#E9E3D0`, polaroids `#FBFAF6`, paint marker `#F4F0E6` on the wall,
  orange marker circles and strikes. Credits in grey-green `#B8C4BC`. Text ink `#17140F`.
- **Fonts** (files in `film/assets/fonts/`): Schibsted Grotesk 800-900 (headlines on cards: "UI", "UX", "Good design doesn't..."),
  Permanent Marker (sticky notes and wall words: "UX RESEARCH?", "SAY / DO"), Courier Prime (typed lines on index cards and source
  credits), Hanken Grotesk, Caveat (polaroid captions). Carousels add DM Mono labels.
- **Two-colour logic**: yellow notes for one side, orange for the other (UI vs UX, generative vs evaluative, say vs do). One accent: orange.
- **Real sources shown**: NN/g definition of UX (highlighted line), Don Norman's NN/g bio, Maze guide, IxDF, each with a Courier
  "Screenshot: ..." credit. Polaroids of her (frames from her recording, never flipped).
- The look carried into everything: talking-head graphics, shorts, teaser, thumbnails set 1, both carousels.

## The motion film

- **Length** 12:04.6 (voice 719.5 s after cuts from 775.5 s raw; 0.6 s intro, 4.5 s outro). 30 fps.
- **Scenes**: 49 boards on one long wall, 48 cuts (`film/data/transitions.json`), written in 3 scene files (`film/scenes/s1.js` opening
  + UI/UX, `s2.js` UX to categories, `s3.js` three pairs to the close) on a shared kit `wall.js`. About one board every 14.8 s.
- **How a new section starts**: the camera travels along the wall to a fresh patch, and a Permanent Marker heading is written on
  the green ("WHY?", "WHO IS A UX RESEARCHER?", "SO MANY WAYS TO CATEGORISE IT", "HOW IS IT DONE?", "WILL AI REPLACE UX RESEARCH?").
  Pairs are numbered on cards ("01 / 03 Generative").
- **Transitions** (measured from transitions.json): 28 camera travels along the wall with the paint-marker line drawing on with the
  move; 17 camera moves inside the same board (0.8 s inOutQuart with motion blur) for "same topic, next idea"; 1 dive (the opening
  camera sinks through the strata into the wall); 1 pull far back over the whole wall at "So that's UX research"; 1 fly-in to the
  closing card (1.2 s).
- **Signature moves**: notes slapped on with a spring; index cards typed on line by line; paint-marker circles and strikes drawn over
  notes (categories board, "screens" struck out in the closing line); a slow push on every hold; punch-ins inside boards.
- **Content devices**: yellow vs orange sticky-note pairs, five sticky-note people with one in orange, a generic video-call frame,
  bar charts for "numbers", a "slow work: a transcript" card for AI, her polaroid "still a student" for the career part.
- **9:16**: recomposed, not cropped (cards stack vertically). On the sheet many 9:16 frames are mostly empty green with small content.
- **Fixed in the voice**: "back in the eighties" cut (Don Norman joined Apple in the early 1990s); retakes and "Right?" cut.

## What to beat next time

1. **The motion film never reached 8** (6.3). Critic: about 9 holds of 4-7 s with only a slow push (56, 291, 373, 427, 563, 571,
   635, 649, 655 s); 12 minutes of one board grammar (notes + cards on green), punch-ins were the only variety; the opening dive has
   2-3 s still stretches.
2. **9:16 is the weak size.** Talking-head 9:16 scored 4: the wall camera punches in and cuts text at the frame edges, near-empty
   wall frames (a lone "03"), empty card bodies. Film text in 9:16 boards is 20-30 px, under the 32 px rule. The 9:16 sheet shows
   many mostly-empty green frames.
3. **Shorts sh1-sh6 were never re-scored** after fixes (4-6 before). Small film text inside 9:16 half panels; caption over her
   forehead at the seam; sh12 and sh13 end flat.
4. **Teaser ended at 6**: critics wanted a louder cold open and harder card slams (applied, not re-scored).
5. **Thumbnails**: three sets and none was posted; she built her own from our best part. Sticky notes looked flat (no paper
   texture), phone props too small at 168 px. Start from her own photo with a one-colour wash and few, huge props.
6. Facts: catch the speaker's slips early (the "eighties" decade) and keep unverified sources (the Medium author) uncredited until checked.
