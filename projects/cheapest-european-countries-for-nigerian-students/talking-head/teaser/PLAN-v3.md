# Teaser v3 plan (2026-11-12): her lines in play order

Her rule (TEASER-PLAYBOOK 0a): one relatable stretch as the hook -> the idea in one line -> 3-5 countries, each a complete
mini-thought she names out loud, a title card each -> her takeaway -> the ending. Raw-recording times; every edge sits before
the next word (word times, checked with a transcript of the new sound). v2 is in ../../archive/teaser-v2/.

Frame 1: STUDY IN EUROPE / THE REAL COST (big, over her)

1. Hook [0.05-5.36] + [7.37-18.05] (the full edit's opening cut 5.36-7.37 is the topic said twice)
   "If you're looking for cheap, affordable countries in Europe to study in, someone has probably told you that Germany is
   free. And for tuition, at most public universities, it is. But you also have the whole proof of funds thing."

2. The idea [41.56-50.30] + [51.00-62.45]
   "So when I say a country in Europe is cheap to study, I mean affordability, like first of all in the tuition fee and also
   proof of funds. If you don't know what proof of funds is, is the money you have to show the embassy or the immigration
   office to prove that you can live and sustain yourself in the country where you want to go and study."

Card: GERMANY (Country 01)

## Cold read (someone who sees only the teaser)
- Hook: one idea (Germany is free... but proof of funds). Every line stands.
- The idea: "cheap" = tuition + proof of funds, and what proof of funds is. The hook's "proof of funds thing" is explained
  right after it.
- Each country: the card names it, she names it in her first words, and each bite has its setup and its number. No "it" or
  "that" whose subject was cut. Austria's "that list" is introduced in the same sentence.
- What it leaves for the full video: which country is cheapest, the age rule, Italy, Estonia, Norway and Finland.
- Length: her lines about 104 s before pause tightening + 4 cards of 0.8 s + the 6.5 s ending, about 110-115 s.
