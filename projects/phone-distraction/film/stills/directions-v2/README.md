# phone-distraction: three new graphic directions (2026-10-17)

Why: she said the film was "text, text, no imagery, no storytelling" and that the off-black ground is "so dull". All three directions below:
- tell the story with real photographs, objects and real sources;
- have one device that comes back across the whole video;
- use a lively ground;
- carry her new type as it is (pale-yellow three-part captions #F4EFB4, heavy yellow keyword stacks #F3EC7A).

The current film (`film/scenes`) is untouched.

**Overview:** `overview.jpg` (each row is 6 frames in 16:9 plus 2 in 9:16).

Each folder has:
- `frames/`: 6 at 16:9 and 2 at 9:16
- `contact.jpg`
- a 7-second motion test with her real voice and library sounds (`*_motiontest_16x9.mp4`), plus its sheet
- the seek(t) page that draws everything (`*.html`)

## A · THE HOUSE (`A-house/`): your home screen is a casino floor

**Idea.** "Unpredictable reward is the bedrock of gambling", so the whole video takes place in a casino.
- The phone (a real hand-held phone photo) is the slot machine. Pull-to-refresh is the lever, and the reels land on "nothing", "1 like" or "99+".
- Your attention is a stack of real poker chips.
- B=MAP is dealt as playing cards.
- The book, the TV and the endless feed stand side by side as the "machines" with and without a stopping point.
- The CHI paper sits on a dark casino floor.
- At the end the phone turns grey (the grayscale tip) and the reels stop.

**Look.**
- Flat crimson ground `#A11D33`.
- A deeper red felt band `#6E0F20` along the bottom. The cut-outs pass behind it, so her caption never sits on a hand.
- Cream `#F6EFDC` type, with her yellow as the casino gold.
- Badge red appears only on badges.
- Type: Helvetica Neue Condensed Black for marquee words, DM Mono labels.

**Signature move (test):** three pulls timed to her "sometimes nothing / sometimes small things / sometimes a big thing" (4:08). The screen drags down, the reels spin and land, and the line lights up yellow. The 1930s machine kicks on each pull. Sounds: switch tap, small hit, pop, coin win.

## B · THE RECEIPT (`B-receipt/`): "free" apps, paid for with attention (recommended)

**Idea.** One object runs through the whole video: a till receipt.
- It prints the moment she says "people call it a free service": Instagram $0.00, TikTok $0.00, Facebook $0.00, TOTAL $0.00, PAID WITH: YOUR ATTENTION.
- Every later chapter prints onto the same kind of paper:
  - Fogg's model as an itemised bill (motivation: curiosity, ability: one tap, prompt: a notification = you open the app)
  - pull-to-refresh ×1 ×2 ×3 ×4
  - the EU's four flagged features
- At the end the receipt is torn off over "your attention is an asset".

**Look.**
- Flat electric blue `#1F3BD6` (her own deck blue).
- Real people and objects as halftone print cut-outs with a paper edge: Herbert Simon, B.J. Fogg, the hand-held phone.
- Real sources as paper clippings, marked in her yellow: Simon's 1971 quote, the Fogg book cover, Brichter's patent figure, the Commission press release.
- Receipt in DM Mono, ink `#15161A` on paper `#FBF8EE`.

**Signature move (test):** the receipt prints line by line on her line "people call it a free service, but in a way it is monetised" (1:27). Then the yellow marker swipes "YOUR ATTENTION". Sounds: device click, typewriter hit and click, pop on TOTAL, bell, marker.

## C · FOLLOW THE ATTENTION (`C-focus/`): photographs, and a yellow focus bracket

**Idea.** Documentary colour photographs are the ground: a São Paulo metro carriage of people on their phones, a casino floor, B.J. Fogg lecturing, a vintage TV, a phone glowing at night, a phone held up to a sunset. One object tells the story: a yellow focus bracket labelled YOUR ATTENTION. In every scene something grabs it (a phone in a commuter's hand, a slot screen, the TV's "next episode: next week", the glowing phone). At the end it lets go and opens to the whole horizon.

**Look.**
- Full-bleed photos under one flat even shade (18-45 %).
- A flat translucent strip behind her captions.
- Yellow brackets and yellow stacks set straight on the photo.

**Signature move (test):** the bracket locks onto a commuter's phone on "your phone was…" and opens to the whole carriage on "designed to distract you" (0:00).

## Critics (fresh harsh-motion-director agent each round, default reject)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 7.5 | 6.5 | A: ground made brighter, felt band added, credits added. C: teal dropped (too close to ux-research's green). |
| 2 | 7 | 7.5 | 5 | A: the B=MAP cards dealt as a hand, a disc behind the slot machine, the machine kicks on each pull. B: one photo object per scene, Stanford page dropped, phone added to the EU frame. C rebuilt: colour photos, no text boxes, the bracket only. |
| 3 | 7 | **8** | 5 | Cut-out edges and the chips' reflection fixed, row text shortened, "cashed out" changed to "put it down", the receipt prints faster, sounds varied. C: bracket bigger, locks once, caption strip, less shade. |
| 4 (final) | 7.5 | **8** | 6.5 | Last fixes: A5 notification rows bigger, the EU headline shown in full, the phone rim cleaned. |

Open notes from the final critic:
- **A:** a slight light rim remains on the hand cut-out, and the test holds still from 4.3 s.
- **B:** the same halftone hand is in 4 of 6 frames, and the test holds still from about 4.5 s (in a full build: tear the receipt there).
- **C:** the bracket means less on objects than on phones, and it still risks reading darker than the other two.

## Recommendation: B · The Receipt

It is the only device that is both a picture and the video's argument ("free" apps that you pay for with your attention). It pays off at the end on her closing line. It reads cleanly at phone size, because her pale captions sit on the blue with no strip needed. It is clearly not any of her other looks (green wall, lilac chat, dark query plan, paper deck), and every frame carries a real, credited source.

A is the bolder, more playful second choice, and its slot reel is the most memorable single move. The two can be combined: in B, the pull-to-refresh scene could borrow A's slot reels on the halftone phone.
