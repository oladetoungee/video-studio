Old formats had
stopping points.
The End

Chapters, episodes, a Next page button. Infinite scroll (Aza Raskin, 2006) and autoplay took those stopping points away.

04 / 08
Notifications:
one week on,
one week off.
Alerts on, phone in reach
More inattention and hyperactivity
Alerts off, phone away
Fewer of those symptoms

Notifications are a design choice too. In a CHI 2016 study, 221 people spent one week each way; the “on” week brought more self-reported symptoms.

Kushlev, Proulx and Dunn, “Silence Your Phones”, CHI 2016
