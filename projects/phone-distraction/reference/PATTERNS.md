## What to beat next time

1. **Finish one look before posting.** The posted set mixes Lock screen and Receipt files, and the Receipt film was never exported. Agree the look before the motion film, not after.
2. **Motion film variety.** Look A topped out at 7.5. Most screens used the same grammar (headline left, phone card right), and the critic's last note was about this "screen-template mix". The Receipt rebuild scored 5-6.5 per pass; its asks were:
   - break the "text top-left, hand on the right" layout
   - EU clippings at least 1000 px wide
   - nothing under 30 px
   - fill the empty patches
   - land the marked word at least 8 frames before a cut
   - one hand-and-phone print on at most 4 screens in 21
3. **Graphics that tell a story, not "text, text".** Real photographs, objects and sources from the first draft. This is what made her pick the Receipt.
