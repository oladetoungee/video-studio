## B · CLOCK IN / CLOCK OUT (`B-timecard/`), recommended
**Idea.** Your week is a punch-clock time card: work in and out, class in and out, stamped on your words. It is the video
in one object, and you even say it: "you can just clock out of work and forget about work… for me, I don't think that
5 p.m. is really when work ends."
- Video type: a talking-head explainer with receipts (playbook format "talking head + receipts", #07, #08, #21) and black
  phrase cards (#29) for the big lines.
- **Look.** Charcoal wall `#23201B`, manila card `#E7D7AE`, card rules `#A08A5E`, stamp ink `#1D1A15`. One accent: stamp
  red `#C8102E` (your headband red) for the time or rule that matters. Type: Bebas Neue (headlines and times), Work Sans 600
  (sentences), Cutive Mono (stamps, credits).
- **Motion in numbers.** Stamps land in 0.12 s (scale 1.25 to 1), the card slides in over 0.7 s, words type on your word
  times (0.32 s each, 18 px rise), a push-in to the row you are talking about (x1.22 over 0.7 s, in-out), the punch clock
  runs on only when you say "really".
- **Signature transitions.** A hard cut on your word, then the stamp lands; the card slides out of frame and the next one
  slides in (paper slide sound).
- **Real sources.** Study in Estonia "Working" page (working hours: not limited; around 30 ECTS a semester) and the
  Adult Education Act § 13 on Riigi Teataja (30 calendar days of study leave, 20 paid), cropped only, highlighted on your
  words, with the site and date under them.
- **Talking-head edit:** captions C1 (heavy condensed caps, white with a dark outline, one word in stamp red); your
  real page sits in a manila window on the empty wall beside you; a small time-card stub in the corner counts the tips
  (TIPS 00 / 10 → 10 / 10). Corrections (her rule 2026-11-02): see `b6`, a plain manila screen where the correction types on.
- **Shorts:** top title T3 (an inverse box, here a manila box with black Bebas), captions C1, stamps above your window.
  No timers in shorts.
- **Sound roles.** Stamp (wood hit, typewriter hit), paper slide, card flick, marker for the strike, wall clock tick, camera
  focus on the push-in. No whooshes.
- Files: `frames/b1-b4` (16:9), `b6` (correction), `p1 p2` (9:16), `motion-test.mp4` (raw 8:34.8 to 8:44.4).

## How these were made (to re-render)
- Pages: `<look>/index.html` (plain canvas, every frame a pure function of time), shared `_shared/lib.js`, `fonts.css`.
- Stills: `node _tools/r.mjs shots B-timecard/index.html 1920x1080 b1 b2 b3 b4 b6` (and `1080x1920 p1 p2`).
- Motion test: `node _tools/r.mjs video B-timecard/index.html 1920x1080 514.8 524.4 B-timecard/_silent.mp4`, then
  `python3 _tools/mixtest.py B-timecard 514.8 524.4 B-timecard/_silent.mp4 B-timecard/motion-test.mp4` (cues in
  `sounds.json`), then a gain + limiter pass to -14 LUFS. C needs its footage feeds (see HANDOFF.md).
- Overview: `python3 _tools/overview.py`.
- Once you pick, the look gets rebuilt in the studio engine (film + talking-head layouts) so everything is editable there.
