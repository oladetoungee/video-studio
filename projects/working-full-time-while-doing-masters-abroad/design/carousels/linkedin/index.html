Check if students can work where you study

In Estonia there's no limit on the hours an international student can work. You still have to stay a full-time student, because dropping to part-time can put your residence permit at risk.

studyinestonia.ee, Working · 2 Nov 2026

02
Know your credits before the semester gets busy

In Estonia, full-time means at least 45 ECTS by the end of each study year. Your school may count it its own way, so check your study rules early.
