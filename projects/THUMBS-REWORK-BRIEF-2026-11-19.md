## Do
1. **Audit the current set** against the picks: for each thumbnail write keep / rework / retire with one line why. Keep what
   would sit happily next to the picks. Anything she said she likes is KEEP (fix only what she named).
2. **Retire** = move its folder + PNG out of the studio set into archive/thumbnails-retired-2026-11-19/ (never delete).
3. **Build new ones** until the set is 6-10 strong. Each copies ONE pick (cite "pick1119-NN") in composition, kind of picture
   and type treatment, applied to this video's topic; each a different pick; a mix (face-led in a real scene, a simple device for
   a number, a no-words real moment if it fits, a before/after...). Max 2 "words left + her right" in the final set.
4. **Words**: the thumbnail is a second message next to its title (feeling / twist, 2-5 words, lowercase fine, or none). Plain words
   a stranger understands; no story details from inside the video; search words go in the TITLE.
5. **Titles**: each thumbnail gets 2-3 titles in the picks' voice (first person, conversational, a promise / confession /
   "here's what I found", words people say out loud; main keyword early; 40-60 characters; no hype words, no em-dashes). Add them
   to kit 1a as "New title options (2026-11-19)"; keep the old ones.
6. **Fonts** only from the font board (inspiration/boards/fonts/), in their source colours; readability wins. Every word editable
   in the studio design editor (same content.json system); new folders under design/thumbnails/directions/.
7. **Fresh critic, YouTube-card style**: render each thumbnail with its best title under it like a YouTube home feed card (and the
   thumbnail alone). A NEW general-purpose agent each round sees only those PNGs + the 10 most relevant picks as the "feed" around
   them (no notes, no your opinion): "cold viewer, harsh: per card, what is it about, would you click (1-10 alone / with title),
   which look alike". Up to 3 rounds. Aim for 8. Drop anything under 7 unless it is one she likes.
8. **Sheets**: outputs/_review/thumbnails-2026-11-19.jpg (final set as YouTube cards: thumbnail + title + score, the pick it
   copies beside it) and outputs/_review/thumbnails-2026-11-19-retired.jpg (what went, one-line reason each).
9. **Recommend** one upload pair (thumbnail + title) and a Test & Compare pair in kit section 2 (keep a line saying what the
   previous recommendation was). Do NOT repoint the shorts' ending card (outroCard) thumbnail: list it for her instead.
10. Update STATUS.md (dated block: kept / reworked / retired / new, scores, sheet paths), kit line 3, design/thumbnails/README.md.

## Lessons from the first sets (add-on, 2026-11-19 evening)
- In the critic's feed, use 10 picks OTHER than the ones you copied (a copy next to its original reads as a knock-off).
- Feeling-only words fail when they don't name the topic ("it's not too late.", "Still worth it?"): the feeling must carry the
  topic word too (e.g. "still doomscrolling at 2am?", "job + master's? doable.").
- Øredev photos mid-word read awkward in close-up; prefer the smiling / closed-mouth / gesturing frames, or use them smaller.
