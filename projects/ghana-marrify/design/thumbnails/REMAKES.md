# Remakes of her viral samples (2026-10-06)

Her request: "Take each of these designs I shared with you and just use each of them." So each sample in
`inspiration/viral-samples/` is remade one to one: same design, new subject. Sample 6 (a 503 error screenshot) is not a
thumbnail and is skipped. Samples were cropped out of the YouTube UI and scaled to 1280×720 (`assets/samples/s<N>.jpg`);
all positions below are in that 1280×720 space. Face sizes were measured with Vision on both the sample and her frame
(`tools/faces.swift`), so her face is placed at the sample's face position and size (bigger only where her frame has to
fill the canvas). Her frames: `assets/scene/warm/` (colour balance only, never flipped, never retouched); new frames
92.90, 117.60, 123.40, 87.10, 128.10 (4K) extracted for poses matching the samples.

Topic (stranger's words): Ghana launched an app that lets anyone check whether someone is married or divorced.
