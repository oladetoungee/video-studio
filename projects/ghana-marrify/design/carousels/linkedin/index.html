The checklist
Before you make
personal records
searchable.
01
Return the smallest answer.
“Active marriage on record”. No spouse, no dates.
02
Set access levels.
Individuals see status, approved organisations see more.
03
Check what it means locally.
How does a divorce record land for a woman here?
04
Show people who looked.
A log of every search on your name.
