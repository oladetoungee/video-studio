reciprocity. Built 2026-10-01 to 10-06, posted, raw recording deleted 2026-10-15.

## The look: "Blink"

**Why this look.** The first style frames (v1, archived in `../archive/film-v1/`) were in her deck style: flat paper/ink grounds,
heavy uppercase display type, DM Mono labels, full-bleed accent slides. That was the same look as the reading-stack film. She
turned them down on 2026-10-01: "No two videos must look the same… the whole vibe of the video has to be different." She then
screen-recorded what she liked on skillry.dev (Opus 5.5 gallery) and whatships.com, and said: "always like a clear background",
"look at all the motion", "bring people together", "the eyelid effects". The study is in `../inspiration/STYLE_STUDY.md`. Her
longest dwell was OpenAI "Get ready" (glyph-eyed faces, crowd packing); Cue and Orthogonal gave the blinking mascots on white.

Three directions were built and scored by a fresh critic (faithful / different from reading-stack / fits subject / craft):
A "Blink" 6.5 then 7.5; B "Multiplayer" (named cursors, one continuous take) 6.5 and 6.5; C "Paperwork" 4.5, rejected as "the
v1 deck again". She picked **A "Blink" plus B's best scenes** (the real Marrify search box with faces, the column-lock fold)
on 2026-10-02. Frames: `../film/stills/directions/`.

**What the look is** (kit: `../film/scenes/blink.js`, shared by the film and the moments):
- Ground: one clear white world, `#F4F4F4`, for the whole film. Ink `#141414`. No ground colour changes.
- Colour lives in the characters: blue `#3D8BF2`, red `#EE4B3B`, yellow `#FFC21A`, green `#17C26E`, purple `#8B5CF6`,
  orange `#FF6A1F`, brown `#B7744E` (direction A palette; in the finished film the blue record samples about `#3249D7` and the ground about `#F0F4F3` from a JPEG still).
- Records as characters with eyes: certificate, folder, envelope, stamp, gazette, register, ID card, shop, court. Blinks close
  in 0.05 s and open in 0.09 s; pupils dart to the new thing; happy eyes on good news; eyes down, no gags, on the serious beats.
- Searchers: grey round faces with typographic eyes that swap hard (`o o`, `– –`, `> <`, `* *`), the two eyes one frame apart.
- Crowds pack in from the edges ("searchable by anyone", "the average individual", "building this").
- Type: Instrument Sans 600 headlines, sentence case, top centre, one coloured word; Geist Mono for credits and tiny labels.
  None of the house deck fonts. Scale pass: 16:9 headlines 96 px or more, 9:16 88 px or more.
- Real sources in white cards with a mono credit ("Screenshot · Marrify homepage · 1 Oct 2026"): TechCabal Daily 8 Sep 2026
  highlighted on her words, Marrify's empty search box, news headline crops. Privacy rule: no record list, no real names, nothing
  typed into Marrify's search.
- Her own photos, never flipped: circle cut-outs and polaroid frames from her recording.
- Flags as flat graphics (Ghana, Nigeria, Estonia).

## The motion film

- Length 5:02.7, 1920×1080 and 1080×1920, 30 fps. Her voice edited from 311.8 s to 297.2 s (cutlist `../film/data/cutlist.json`),
  0.5 s intro, 5 s outro.
- **42 screens** (16 in `bscenes1.js`, 0:00-1:39; 26 in `bscenes2.js`, 1:39-5:03), 41 cuts listed in
  `../film/data/transitions.json`. Average about 7 s a screen, with several changes inside each screen.
- Sections are introduced by the **eyelid wipe**: two ink lids close over the frame and open on the next section (seen as the
  black frames at 1:39.6 and 3:37.6 on the sheet). Within a section, the headline stays and the characters change under it.
- Signature moves:
  - pupil dive into the TechCabal drawback and into the minimal answer (the big black circle at 3:04 on the sheet), then an iris
    open on the next scene;
  - springy pops with overshoot (about +25 %);
  - headline entrances rotate between a blur rise, a pop and a hard cut on her word;
  - crowd packing: every record springs in and packs together ("Centralised data", "Yes, please. More of this.");
  - the column-lock fold: a record table locks column by column and folds to "Status" (data minimisation, access levels);
  - a balance beam tipping (open data vs personal records), a courthouse 2003 vs a phone at 3.0 s (consent);
  - a slow push in any hold so nothing sits still over 4 s.
- Ending: "Ghana's Marrify. Centralised data, and what Estonia taught me." with the full cast and @WithGbemi.
- Measured fill after the scale pass: frames under the fill target 16.1 % (16:9) and 17.0 % (9:16), down from 32 % and 31 %.
- She later got a per-screen Light / Dark switch (2026-10-04); every screen has a dark version, none chosen.

## Sound

- Music: Mixkit "A Blue Day" (150) then "Digital Clouds" (175), both Alejandro Magaña, 6 s crossfade, ducked about 12 dB under
  her voice. Moments: one Mixkit track each (m01 175, m02 150, m03 766, m04 443, m05 234, m06 282, m07 135), ducked 10 dB.
- Sound effects: Mixkit, 76 cues in the film, in roles: pop, blink (plastic bubble click), lid (short sweep), dive (air whoosh),
  pen (marker line for highlights), tick (checkbox click), click (column locks), whoosh, impact, crowd, type (keyboard bank).
- Voice chain: high-pass 75 Hz, 3:1 compressor, de-esser; voice at -16 LUFS before the master.
- Master: film -14.0 LUFS, -1.9 dBTP; moments all -14.0 LUFS (m04 -14.1), true peak -1.9 to -2.1 dBTP.
- Sound check (2026-10-11): voice 13.2 dB over music while she speaks; pops on time (median -9 ms). Weak spot: the 8 impacts
  (mk1143) hit about 105 ms early and sit about 18 dB under her voice. Not fixed (she had approved).

## What to beat next time

1. **The film never reached 8.** Set the bar early with stills and do not hand over below it.
2. **Thumbnails need her real photos.** Every taste critic blamed the one dim webcam room. Ask for her photos (holding a phone,
   a prop) before the first round, and start from her samples and Pinterest board. 10b shows what she likes: a clean editorial
   object (clipping, phone) on white, key words highlighted.
3. **No ILLUSTRATION stamps.** This project labelled mockups "ILLUSTRATION"; her rule since 2026-10-14 is to keep mockups generic
   instead and print no disclaimers.
4. **Fill the 9:16 panels.** The main-session review found panels under her face half empty; size content for the panel first.
5. **Impacts on time.** Trim or swap the impact sample so its slam is the first peak, and check every cue with `soundqa.py`.
6. **Cuts open on content.** 18 of 41 cuts opened on about 3 frames of empty ground; land the next scene's first element on the cut.
7. **Carousels in the video's own look.** These used the house deck style while the film used Blink; next time match the video.
8. **Keep what worked:** a look built from her own gallery picks, real credited sources highlighted on her words, a hook on
   screen at frame 0 in every clip, characters that react to her words, and a calm register on the serious beats.
