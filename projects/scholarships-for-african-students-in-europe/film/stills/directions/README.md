## A · STAMPED (`A-stamped/`): a scholarship passport
**Idea.** Each scholarship is a passport page. What it covers lands as entry stamps (TUITION, TRAVEL, VISA, LIVING ALLOWANCE). A round gold stamp means fully funded or money (€1,400 a month), and a machine-readable code line runs along the bottom of each page. Section changes turn the page.
**References:** #29 (black phrase cards), #08 and #21 (a real page as a card with a highlight), #26 (serif captions on a box).
**Look.** Passport burgundy `#5A1424`, page cream `#F2E8D5` with a fine wave pattern, ink `#1A1416`, stamp ink `#8E1B2E`, gold `#C9A043` (fully funded and money only). Fonts: DM Serif Display (headlines), Hanken Grotesk (text), Azeret Mono (labels and stamps).
**Motion.** Stamps slam in on your word: scale 1.38 → 1 in 0.22 s, a small tilt (-6° to +4°), and a blur that clears. The real page slides in from the right over 0.55 s (outQuint) and comes to rest at 1.5°. The highlighter sweeps in 0.5 s. A slow 2 % push runs under everything. Page turns carry the section changes.
**Captions.**
- Talking-head edit: your words in DM Serif on a burgundy box (C4 style).
- Shorts: a mixed-font top title (DM Serif + mono, T7).
**Sound.** Wood hit and typewriter hit for stamps, shutter for the real page, marker for the highlight, coin for the money. No whooshes.
**Motion test:** 108.6-118.9 s, "which includes your tuition, your travel, your visa, and a living allowance… 1,400 euros a month for up to 24 months". Measured at -14.5 LUFS, true peak -1.0 dBTP.
