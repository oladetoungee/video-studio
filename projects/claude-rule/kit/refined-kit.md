### 7d. What worked with strangers
Three fresh cold reviewers (2026-10-12) saw only the titles, thumbnail descriptions, pairs and captions; the design agent ran three more
gates on the built thumbnails (2026-10-13, `design/thumbnails/NOTES.md`).
- **The lesson: the concrete fact (Claude can end your chat if you're cruel) beats every abstract question about consciousness.** Lead
  with it; the consciousness debate is what people stay for. No consciousness-led title (T1, T5, T8) passed 7 alone, so "is claude
  conscious" lives in the description's first line and the tags.
- Round 1 flags acted on: "bans being cruel" overstated a rule about sustained, needless cruelty (rewritten); "tech researcher" is vague
  (now "I research how people use technology"); a made-up newspaper headline looked like a real clipping (removed); "constitution"
  needs a gloss (added); "9pm" and "clock out" are inside references; "BE KIND ANYWAY" read as a wellness channel; #kindness pulls the
  wrong audience.
- Round 2: strangers read the 9pm clock, the "24 HRS" card and the "back at 9am" sign as outages or human burnout, and a chip as
  semiconductors. Her tiredness idea moved to the film, SH5 (LinkedIn first) and the community post.
- Round 3: the printed policy page, wrench, gavel, flat heartbeat, shield and "NOT ALIVE" stamp scored 5-6 and were rebuilt into the
  "consequence" family (an ended chat).
- Built-thumbnail gates (2026-10-13): mouths mid-word were replaced by closed-mouth frames; T2 "Will Ban" dropped (read as account bans);
  07 "should AI hang up" and 09 "talk nice or else" cut.
- Dropped ideas (so nobody rebuilds them): the printed policy page held up, wrench, gavel, flat heartbeat, brain + shield, "NOT ALIVE"
  stamp, door, 9pm clock, "IS CLAUDE OVERWORKED?", "BACK AT 9AM", "WHY PROTECT THE CHIP?", the made-up newspaper headline, "HUMAN
  RULES, AI?", "CLAUDE ≠ HUMAN", "JUST A TOOL", "NOT HUMAN", "BE KIND ANYWAY".
- Not adopted: reviewer suggestions that overstate ("Anthropic Now Bans Being Cruel", "Your AI can now hang up on you" as if new).
