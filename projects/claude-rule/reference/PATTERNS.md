## The look: "The Thread"

The whole video is built like one chat on a phone. Her lines arrive as her chat messages, Anthropic's and Suleyman's real
pages arrive from the other side as cards, the model never speaks, and sections end with "This conversation has ended".
She chose this look on 2026-10-13 from three directions (A The Thread, B The Fine Print, C The Slope; see
`../film/stills/directions/README.md`). The final film is A, plus B's "two voices" (their words in a serif, hers in her
bubbles) and C's tilting plank as one set piece.

- **Grounds**: lilac `#CDBFF3` (taken from the top she wore in the recording) for most screens; night `#141119` for big-type
  moments and the end card. Count from `film/scenes`: 57 screens, 49 lilac, 7 night (plus the end card logic). The lilac
  matches her top, so in the talking-head edit the motion panels feel like an extension of her.
- **Her bubbles**: ink `#15131D`, text `#F4F1FA`, Schibsted Grotesk 500, 66 px in 16:9 after review (first 58), radius 34
  with a 10 px tail corner, column 1340 px wide. Key words turn lilac as she says them; words not yet spoken show faint.
- **Source cards**: white, 2 px `#A99BD6` edge, a highlighter `#B9A5F5` laid over the exact phrase, a small IBM Plex Mono
  credit ("Screenshot · anthropic.com · 8 Oct 2026").
- **Fonts**: Schibsted Grotesk 500/700/800 (bubbles, big type, breakouts), IBM Plex Mono 400/500 (small labels, at least 30 px),
  Newsreader 400/600 serif (other people's words: Anthropic's policy, the constitution, Suleyman). Thumbnails used Schibsted 900.
- **Stickers**: words on black or white tape strips, slightly rotated (see the "Claude" stickers and the plank piles).
- **No logos**: no Claude or Anthropic logo, no real Claude interface; chats are generic. "ILLUSTRATION" labels were used at
  first and then all removed after her 2026-10-14 rule (no disclaimers on ready-to-post work).

## The motion film

- Length 4:28.7 (voice 262.2 s after tightening from 272.3 s, plus a 0.5 s intro and a 6 s outro).
- **57 screens** (`addT` calls in `film/scenes/scenes1.js` and `scenes2.js`), **56 cuts** on the cut board
  (`film/data/transitions.json`), all hard cuts (no dissolves). About half the running time is the chat; the rest is
  full-frame screens.
- How sections are introduced: a fresh "New chat" (empty input row, then her first message lands on the cut) or a hard cut
  to big type on her opening word ("And it's even interesting", "But overall"). Sections close with "This conversation has
  ended" on a tape-stop sound.
- Signature moves:
  - **Breakout**: one word she lands on flies out of her bubble and fills about 75 % of the frame with a white marker behind it
    ("cruel.", "Hey, I'm tired.", "an offender?", "No, it's not.", "This conversation has ended"). Out-expo, 15 frames.
  - **Real page fills the frame** on a camera-shutter sound, then the highlighted phrase **lifts into serif type**
    (policy rule at 0:42, constitution at 1:02, Suleyman's essay at 2:38).
  - **The meter**: "not sure ... might be conscious" with a dot sliding on her reading of Anthropic (0:31).
  - **Stickers on "Claude"**: her human words (human behaviour, human consciousness, a form of humanity) stick on, then fall
    off with gravity and "Claude is a tool." lands (1:21-1:34).
  - **The sum**: 48,213 x 9,771 typed in mono, the answer counting in lilac (1:41).
  - **Struck-through questions** (her three "Do I want to abuse it?" questions, 0:17).
  - **The plank** (TOOL ... HUMAN): words pile up, the plank tilts on "slippery slope", the pile slides off; it returns at the
    end (2:16-2:32 and 4:11).
  - **Numbered list** (01 give a machine human consciousness, 02 impose..., 03 make it subject to rules) and the **if-then chain**
    (could be conscious → could be abused → could be tired or frustrated?).
  - **Clock chat**: 9:00 AM requests piling up, then "9pm or 10pm" on night.
  - **Day and night**: the ground flips on her words "day" and "night" (3:40).
  - End: "And I don't know, man." on night, the big "This conversation has ended", then the @WithGbemi end card typing on.
- Motion blur on moves (shutter 180, up to 24 px).
- 9:16 is recomposed, not cropped: chats stack in the column, real pages show whole lines (page text then about 26 px wide at
  1080, so the serif lift right after does the reading work).

## Talking-head full edit (4:21.8, 16:9 and 9:16)

Built in `../talking-head/moments/full.js` with the studio's layout menu. Her footage only scaled and cropped, never
flipped. Retakes cut (4 cuts in `talking-head.json`), pauses longer than 0.42 s tightened.

Layout calls in `full.js`: talk (her full frame, zoom/punch-in) 9, keyword overlays 15, shout (big words over her) 5,
behind (words behind her head) 3, stack 2, pops 3, half and half 8, corner cam over film 3, film cutaway 6, card 3,
cut-out sticker over a real page 2, highlight 3, glitch 1 ("SLIPPERY").

**Measured mix** (the 16:9 contact sheet, 87 frames, one every 3 s):

| What is on screen | Frames | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Her full frame with words on top (keyword, shout, stack, pops, behind) | 28 | 32 % |
| Her full frame, nothing added | 16 | 18 % |
| Half and half (film panel + her) | 15 | 17 % |
| Film full frame with her in a small circle (corner cam) | 11 | 13 % |
| Film or card full frame, without her | 10 | 11 % |
| Her cut-out over a real page (constitution, Suleyman) | 7 | 8 % |

So she is visible in about 89 % of sampled frames, and something is added on top in about 80 %. After her 2026-10-14
note ("some shots are missing her video") a rule went into `common.js`: a film-only cutaway longer than 6 s shows about
3 s full frame, then she comes back (16:9 as a circle over the film, 9:16 as film on top 1130 px and her below).
In 9:16 the half-and-half (film top, her bottom) is the main layout; the cut-out stands under the page.

## What to beat next time

1. **Film below the bar**: 7.0 after 3 rounds. Open notes: plain chat stretches of 8-10 s (54-67 s, 96-106 s, 190-201 s in
   round 3), "more image-led screens" (no photos or objects beyond her words, pages and drawn shapes), sound hits partly off
   (a quarter of cues' first peak early; 23 of 80 off by more than a frame or two).
2. **Talking-head edit** scored 6/6 and was never re-scored. Her cut-out covers part of the constitution and Suleyman pages
   (1:03, 2:39); screenshot body text is small; film fragments like "But I" (1:45) and "But overall," (4:09) show up alone;
   the dark "A what-if" card at about 2:58 sits nearly empty while it types on. 18 % of frames still show her with nothing added.
3. **Shorts** scored 4-7. Weakest sh5 (4). Open: sh3 slow first line, sh1 meter hold about 7 s, captions repeat the chat
   bubbles in sh1/sh2 (the same words twice on screen), Suleyman screenshot text small in sh4.
4. **Teaser** has no trailer grammar (no title cards, no visible transitions) and its 16:9 captions are faint over her
   lilac top; the slope insert's "HUMAN" label is cut at the right edge in 16:9 (see the slope-insert still).
5. **Thumbnails**: the handheld mic sits at her chin in every video frame; her own photos would beat them. Thumbnail 22 goes
   stale after the date it names. Keep news dates off thumbnails that must last.
6. Carry over: the lilac taken from her own top (panels feel like part of her shot), the chat-as-structure idea, breakouts
   from a spoken word, real pages with highlight then serif lift. Do not reuse the look itself (house rule: every video
   gets its own look).
