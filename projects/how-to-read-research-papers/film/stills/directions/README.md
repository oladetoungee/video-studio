## The three looks

### A · The Margin (recommended)
**The idea:** Keshav's real paper ("How to Read a Paper", 2007) is the object we carry through the whole video, and we mark it up the way you said you do now: fluorescent highlighter on the lines that matter, blue-pen notes in the margin, numbered tabs 1-2-3 clipped to the edge for the passes. Every receipt in the video is a real page: Keshav's lines, Kelly Caine's abstract, the Cochrane abstract, Zotero.
- **Video type:** talking head + receipts, with one running example (the same paper all video). From the references: #08/#21/#34 (a screenshot as the receipt, with a marker highlight), #03/#25 (one running example), #20 (hand-written notes over a paper).
- **Look:** flat graphite `#2F3136`, paper `#FBFAF4`, highlighter `#F3EE2E` (only ever on paper or on your captions), blue pen `#2447C9`. Type: Newsreader 800 for headlines (the key word in italic, never on a yellow bar), Caveat Bold for your handwriting, the paper's own type for receipts.
- **Motion in numbers:** the paper slides in over 0.75 s (out-expo, blur 6 to 0 px). The highlighter runs each line in 0.10 s, staggered 0.11 s, and lands on her words. Pen strokes draw over 0.22-0.4 s. Handwriting writes on left to right over 0.5 s. Tabs clip on with a 0.08 s stagger. A slow 6 % push runs over 5.5 s, so nothing sits still for more than 3 s.
- **Signature transitions:** the camera travels across the same page to the next marked passage; the highlighter sweep wipes the frame; a page turn moves to page 2 (pass two and three).
- **Captions:** full edit: condensed Helvetica caps (HN Condensed Black 76-80 px) with the key word on a highlighter block (catalogue C1/C2). Shorts: the same captions; the top title is a paper card ("Is this research paper *any good?*", T3 inverse box drawn as paper), and it swaps to the real receipt as the heading when she cites one (T9). No timers in shorts.
- **Sound:** paper slide, marker squeak (`marker-line`), pen scribble, pencil, pop, tap, card place. No whoosh in the test (0 of 8 cues). Music bed Mixkit 167 at -24 dB, ducked under her voice. -13.8 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP.
- **Frames:** `A-margin/frames/` a1 opening ("not a novel"), a2 pass one (Keshav's real line), a3 the five Cs (Keshav's list + her plain words), a4 **in-edit correction** (Caine studied every paper *at CHI* 2014), p1 the three passes (9:16), p2 "Print it out so you can mark it up" with a Pexels photo (9:16). Motion test: `A-margin/motion-test.mp4`.

### B · Typeset
**The idea:** the video itself is laid out like a research paper, in LaTeX's Computer Modern: a title block, your opening as the abstract, numbered sections, "Figure 1" charts, a "Table 1" for the three checks, green citation brackets [1] that open into the real source, and an **Erratum** box for the correction. In the talking-head edit you are "Figure 2", with your live caption set as the figure caption.
- **Video type:** talking head + a slide behind/beside her (the page), from #32/#33 (slides/TV behind her) and #26 (the further-reading end card, here a real reference list).
- **Look:** page `#FBFAF6`, ink `#141414`, citation green `#0E7C3A` (the only colour). Computer Modern Serif / Typewriter. Two-column grid, 160 px margins.
- **Motion:** lines "compile" in top to bottom (clip reveal 0.35 s each, 0.25 s apart); the page scrolls up 330 px over 1 s (in-out quart) to the next section; citation brackets pop in from 150 % to 100 % over 0.25 s.
- **Captions:** full edit: serif captions (C4 style, Computer Modern) as figure captions under her. Shorts: letterbox (T8) with a serif title above and serif captions below.
- **Sound:** typewriter hit and return bell, chalk line, page turn, soft clicks. No whoosh. -13.8 LUFS.
- **Frames:** `B-typeset/frames/` b1 abstract + sections, b2 Figure 1 (time per pass, from Keshav), b3 **Erratum** (the CHI 2014 correction + Caine's abstract), b4 Table 1 (the three checks in her words), p1 Definition (pass three), p2 References with ticks. Motion test: `B-typeset/motion-test.mp4`.

### C · Copy Room
**The idea:** the photocopier. You print your papers now, so each pass is a scan: a green scan bar sweeps the page; pass one shows only the title, abstract, headings and conclusion, the rest stays as grey ghost lines; three copies of one paper get fuller pass by pass.
- **Video type:** talking head + black phrase cards (#29) + words behind her (#19).
- **Look:** toner black `#0F0F0F`, copy paper `#F1F0EB` with a fixed (not moving) toner grain, one fluoro green `#3DF07B`. Space Grotesk Bold caps + Space Mono labels.
- **Captions:** one word at a time, Space Grotesk caps on a paper chip (C6); shorts top title as a full-frame title flash (T6).
- **Sound:** air zoom, tech slide, bass hit, hard shutter, device clicks. No whoosh. -13.8 LUFS.
- **Frames:** `C-copyroom/frames/` c1 pass-one scan, c2 three copies, c3 printer (Pexels, photocopied look), c4 "Who funded it?" with the Cochrane abstract, p1 black phrase card "Ten minutes can save you a whole evening.", p2 twelve people (9:16). Motion test: `C-copyroom/motion-test.mp4`.
