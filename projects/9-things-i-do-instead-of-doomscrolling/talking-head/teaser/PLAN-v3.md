each with a title card -> her takeaway -> the ending). Raw-recording times; every start / end sits in a pause between words.
v2 (18 single lines from all over, 88.8 s) is in build/before-fullkit/teaser.js + talking-head.json.

## Cold read (someone who sees only the teaser)
- Hook: one idea (why you can't stop): the question, then the stopping-cue explanation whole. "the infinite scroll" is named
  in the same sentence it is used; "that" in "doesn't have that" = the stopping cue just described.
- The idea says what the video is: the things she does instead of doomscrolling.
- Each example is named by its card and is a complete thought (reading: where the book goes; the bike: why and what it felt
  like; thrifting: the hunt). Her "Number one on my list" intros are left out because the cards name them.
- 8-10 close: "instead of doomscrolling, talk to people" -> "you can't get that from social media" -> "It can never beat
  genuine connection". "It" = social media, said just before.
- Length: her lines 106.4 s + 4 cards x 0.8 s + the 6.5 s ending = about 116 s.
