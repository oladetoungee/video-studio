Why it's so hard to stop
Your feed has no end

A book gives you the end of a chapter and a show gives you the end of an episode. Infinite scroll on Instagram or TikTok has neither, so your brain never gets a natural stopping cue.

Source: Rixen et al., 2023
02 / 13
My one rule
Replace it with something easy

For most of us, doomscrolling is what we do when we're tired, or when we don't want to do something more difficult. If the new habit takes too much effort, you'll end up back on your phone.

03 / 13
01
Read

If I'm going out, you'll barely see me without a book. I noticed I picked up my phone every time I ate, so now I read while I eat.

04 / 13
02
Let yourself be bored

Try it in small places, like waiting for your kettle to boil or standing at a bus stop. It feels uncomfortable at first, and then you get okay with just being bored.

05 / 13
03
Talk to people

It can be small talk with someone who isn't even a friend. I'll take awkward talking with people over scrolling on social media any time, any day.

06 / 13
04
Make friends as an adult
Our parents at the school gate: "My child is going to this school, your child is going to this school." Boom, friends.

That's how our parents' generation in Nigeria made friends. I think we can be a bit more intentional about it too.

07 / 13
05
Pick up something new

This summer I learned to ride a bike. I also hunt for bags at thrift stores now, and I'm still failing at banana bread. It doesn't have to make money.

08 / 13
06
Long-form, on purpose

This one can still be on a screen. Watch a long YouTube video or read a long article, and give it your attention instead of leaving it on in the background.

09 / 13
07
Do something physical

Go for a walk without your phone, or do ten push-ups when you want to pick it up. I'm very physically lazy, but I've tried it a few times and it works.

10 / 13
08
Pray, then write it down

Even a few minutes of prayer or meditation in the time you'd have spent scrolling. Writing doesn't have to be profound. A brain dump is enough.

11 / 13
09
Call your people

Call your mom, your dad, your siblings, your friends. Social media can never beat the genuine connection you get from the people in your life.
