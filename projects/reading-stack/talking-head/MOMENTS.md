# Talking-head moments: reading stack

Seven moments from her 18-minute recording (`../kit/raw-video.mov`). Each one is self-contained: a hook in the first two seconds and a payoff line at the end.

Graphics run the whole way through, measured by `composite.mjs check`:
- a new graphic event every 1-3 s
- no stretch over 2 s with nothing added
- no graphics hold over 3 s without a cut or camera move
- no empty panels
- no text past a panel edge
- nothing over her face

**Times**
- **Source in/out:** seconds on the raw video.
- **Length:** the finished clip, after jump cuts, plus a 0.7 s hold at the end.
- **Beat times:** on the finished clip.

**Trims (`data/edl.json`)**
- Every pause longer than 0.42 s is cut down to 0.24 s.
- The listed retakes and asides are removed.
- Cuts land in silences on frame boundaries. Where there was no silence, the cut point was chosen by transcribing candidate joins.
- Every export was transcribed again to confirm no words were clipped.

**Her footage**
- It is never flipped or colour-changed, only cropped.
- Where a book cover has to read, it appears as a correctly oriented polaroid.
- While she holds a book at chest height (it reads backwards in the mirrored source), the beat uses a lower band, split or full card whose panel covers most of it.

**Layout vocabulary**
- **Beside:** flat plates of heavy type next to her face (16:9 left third; 9:16 under her chin).
- **Split:** a flat panel. In 16:9 it is on the left (880 px) and she is reframed to the right. In 9:16 it runs from y 1110 down; she is drawn smaller above it and the captions move to the panel line.
- **Band (16:9):** a flat panel over the bottom 34% of the frame.
- **Card:** full frame while her voice continues.
- **Punch:** the virtual camera cuts or pushes in on the 4K source.

**Sound in every moment**
- Her voice is cleaned: high-pass 80 Hz, light FFT noise reduction, de-ess, gentle 3:1 compression and a small presence lift.
- A Mixkit music bed sits under her, ducked 15 dB under speech and 9 dB in pauses. It fades in over 0.5 s and out over 1.4 s.
- SFX land on graphic hits:
  - pop: plates
  - whoosh: panels
  - card: cards and polaroids
  - tick: list rows
  - mark: strikes and underlines
  - hit: punchline cards
  - keys: typing
- Master: −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
