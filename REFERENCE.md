# REFERENCE: the standard to beat

Last updated 2026-11-01. One page across every finished, posted project. Each project keeps its patterns in
`projects/<slug>/reference/` (PATTERNS.md + one contact sheet per finished video + full-size stills of the strongest moments).

On 2026-11-01 the finished videos (MP4s, 16 GB) were moved to the Bin at her request (`~/.Trash/finished-videos-2026-11-01/<slug>/`, same folder layout).
Kept: every .srt, every thumbnail / carousel PNG and PDF, kits, transcripts, voice, film sources and sound stems, talking-head manifests and edits, design files.
Motion films can still be re-rendered in the studio. Talking-head edits, shorts and teasers cannot (the raw recordings are gone).

Read the PATTERNS.md of the closest past project before starting a new one, then make something that beats it (and looks different: every video gets its own look).

## Our standard so far

**Best scores to beat**
- **Motion film:** 8.0, database-indexes. Next best: october-attacks 7.6 and phone-distraction 7.5.
- **Short:** 9, ux-research sh14. Several shorts at 8-8.5 in phone-distraction and ux-research.
- **Thumbnail:** 8.5, october-attacks 13, 14 and 18. Her own thumbnails (database-indexes, ux-research) are the real bar.
- **Carousels:** 8-8.1, claude-rule, ux-research LinkedIn and phone-distraction.
- **Full talking-head edit:** 7.5, october-attacks. **Teaser:** 6.5, database-indexes and october-attacks.

**What consistently worked**
- One idea behind the look, with one accent colour that always means the same thing (yellow = the query path, red badge, the October calendar).
- Diagrams that ARE the topic: B-tree walk, scan counter, research wall, the chat thread.
- Real sources shown and highlighted on her exact words, with credits.
- Hard cuts on her words instead of the same transition everywhere.
- Varied sound effects with few or no whooshes. Every master at -14 LUFS.
- Shorts that open on a strong stand-alone idea, with her outro clip at the end.

**Recurring weak spots (fix these first next time)**
- **Teasers stall at 6-6.5:** too much of her talking alone, weak trailer structure.
- **Full edits score about 6, and their 9:16 versions worse (4-5.5):** text cut at the edges, the face inset covering cards, film text too small in half panels.
- **Long films lose variety:** dead holds, and the same layout repeated for 10+ minutes (ux-research, reading-stack).
- **Fixes without a fresh score:** first-round shorts and full edits were fixed and never scored again. Always score again after fixes.
- **Thumbnails are capped by webcam frames** (mic at her chin, dim room). She made her own for the two strongest, so photos of her without a mic are needed.
- **Facts missing from the record:** the posted title and thumbnail are missing for most projects, and the labels she edits in the studio drift away from talking-head.json. Record what she posted, with the YouTube link, in STATUS.md at posting time.

Contact sheets: `python3 studio/tools/refsheets.py <slug>` (needs the full ffmpeg build at /opt/homebrew/opt/ffmpeg-full/bin/ffmpeg for the timestamp text). Make a project's reference/ this way before its finished videos are binned.
